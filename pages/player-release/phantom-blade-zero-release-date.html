Phantom Blade Zero Release Date: October 28 Launch, Delay History & Preorder Status

Category: Live Stats & Roadmap • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Phantom Blade Zero launches October 28, 2026 on Steam, Epic Games Store, and PS5 (worldwide unlock rolls into Oct 29 UTC per SteamDB) — delayed from its original September 9 slot via a creative director letter. Pre-purchase is live, and the game has already climbed to #1 on Steam's best-seller chart.

The Date, Officially

October 28, 2026 is the date printed on the Steam page, with SteamDB's unlock history indicating a worldwide rollout moment slipping into October 29 UTC — the standard regional midnight cascade. Platforms: Steam, Epic Games Store, and PS5 (PS5 Pro supported). One structural fact from official promo material: the PS5 holds a 12-month console exclusivity window, meaning other consoles — if they ever come — are a late-2027 conversation at the earliest.

The Delay: September 9 → October 28

The original September 9, 2026 date was pushed via a creative director letter, with the stated logic being a brutally packed September window (a calendar collision our own Phase-0 research documented extensively). S-GAME chose polish and breathing room over a crowded month — and then validating the move, pre-purchase momentum carried the title to #1 on Steam's best-seller chart per launch-window reporting.

Preorder Status & Pricing Snapshot

Pre-purchase opened with the October trailer wave. Pricing snapshots from the official S-GAME preorder reveal: CN region standard ¥268 / deluxe ¥298; Hong Kong listings around RM189/239. Standard edition extras include early cosmetics (Treasure Bowl accessory, Legacy appearance); the deluxe tier adds Wulin Legend and Mechanical Puppet appearance sets plus the art book and OST. Regional pricing varies — treat your own storefront's checkout as ground truth.

Post-Launch Roadmap: Deliberately Unannounced

No DLC schedule, season structure, or update cadence has been published. A 12-month console-exclusivity clause is the only long-dated official commitment on record. As a strictly single-player title, the post-launch conversation — if any — is likely expansion-shaped rather than seasonal. Nothing here is speculation; when S-GAME speaks, this page updates.
